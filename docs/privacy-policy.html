Privacy Policy

Soapy Escape

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Soapy Escape does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no tracking, and no internet connection required to play.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party software development kits of any kind. It contains no networking code, so no information can leave your device even in principle.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so you can pick up where you left off. That save file contains only:

  • Which levels you have completed
  • How many stars you earned on each level
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial and the intro carousel

This file never leaves your device and is not readable by us or by anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it permanently.

Children's privacy

Soapy Escape is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no external links, and no way to communicate with other players.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because there is no data to share. No third-party service receives any information about you or your use of the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Soapy Escape ever collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here with a new date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to soapyescape@gmail.com.
